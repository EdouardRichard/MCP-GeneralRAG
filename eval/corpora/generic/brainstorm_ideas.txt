Product brainstorm — unfiltered ideas for the knowledge platform (Q3 2026).

Idea 1: Personal knowledge graph. Let users declare links between their own
notes (note A references note B) and surface those links during retrieval.
Risk: manual linking is high-friction; extraction heuristics may misfire.

Idea 2: Meeting-to-minutes pipeline. Upload an audio recording, transcribe it,
and auto-file a structured summary into the team knowledge base with speaker
attribution. Risk: transcription quality on domain jargon; privacy review
needed before launch.

Idea 3: Reading queue curator. A weekly digest that ranks saved bookmarks by
topic relevance to current projects and prunes stale entries. Cheap to build,
mostly a ranking problem we already have infrastructure for.

Idea 4: Cross-language search. A Chinese query returns evidence from English
documents with the matching passage highlighted and translated inline. The
multilingual embedding model already supports this; the UI work is the main
cost.

Idea 5: Evidence freshness badge. Every evidence card shows how old the
underlying document is, so stale decisions are visible at a glance. Almost
trivial to implement from existing metadata; high trust payoff.

Parking lot: semantic spreadsheet formulas (too far from the retrieval core),
voice-first mobile client (no team capacity this quarter).
